Civic Checker

Drop a car photo. The app finds every vehicle and checks which ones are a 10th-gen Honda Civic (2016–2021).

Work in progress: very accurate on photos where one car fills most of the picture. On busy street photos (cars far away, many local models such as Perodua and Proton) it can still mistake other cars for a Civic, and I'm improving that next.

Drag & drop a photo here, or tap to choose one
Photos are checked in memory and never saved.

    How it works

    1. A pretrained YOLOv8 detector finds every car, truck and bus.
    2. Each vehicle is cropped and checked by my own EfficientNetV2-S classifier (Keras 3, PyTorch backend), trained on ~720 photos I collected. Green = Civic, red = not Civic.
    3. Both models run as ONNX on a small free server.

    97.7% (42/43) on a fixed set of unseen close-up photos. Known weakness: local cars it never saw in training (e.g. Perodua, Proton, vans) can be wrongly called Civic. The first check after a quiet period can take about a minute while the free server wakes up.
